# Proposed solution

The proposed solution should technically explain the final product, method or result. The DIA v2 guide asks for functionalities, screenshots, diagrams and a way to test the development: deployment, executable, installer or demonstration video when applicable {cite:p}`dia_documentacion_tfg_v2`.

This section must connect requirements and outcome. It is not enough to show screens; explain the relevant technical decisions, main components and how the solution responds to the objectives.

## What should be clear

Include, depending on the TFG:

- overview of the solution;
- system architecture;
- main components and responsibilities;
- data or usage flow;
- implemented functionalities;
- screenshots or diagrams with explanation;
- technical decisions and discarded alternatives;
- repository, demo, deployment, installer or video link;
- tests or evidence of operation.

Diagrams should be referenced in the text and have captions. A screenshot without explanation has little value; a screenshot showing how a requirement is solved does.

## From proposal to result

Explain how the solution evolved: problems found, decisions made, scope changes, limitations and particularities of the final version. This often separates a mature report from a superficial description.

If the solution includes AI, external models or APIs, document what the author controls and what depends on third-party services. If data is used, explain its origin, treatment and limitations. If there is deployment, state how it can be reproduced or tested.

## Validation evidence

Close the solution with evidence: tests, metrics, user validation, comparison with requirements or result analysis. If the detail is large, keep the synthesis here and move full tables, cases or logs to the relevant annex.
