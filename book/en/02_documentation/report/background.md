# Background and current situation

This block has two functions: explaining the background of the problem and describing the current situation. The DIA v2 guide separates both points: background gives the theoretical or empirical basis of the domain, while the current situation presents a state of the art, market study or existing solutions depending on the TFG {cite:p}`dia_documentacion_tfg_v2`.

## Background

The background should explain the non-computing domain when needed. If the TFG deals with education, health, business management, heritage, sports or scientific research, this section should give the committee the minimum basis needed to understand the problem.

Include:

- essential domain concepts;
- relevant previous studies or works;
- limitations in previous solutions;
- needs of users, organizations or processes;
- company context, only when applicable and allowed.

Theory should serve the TFG. There is no need to write a manual for every technology used; explain the prior knowledge that the committee needs in order to understand your decisions.

## Current situation

Compare existing alternatives: similar applications, reference tools, commercial products, repositories, papers, regulations or common practices. If technological evolution matters, describe it briefly, but avoid a long history with no connection to your solution.

This section should rely on references. Do not simply claim that no adequate solution exists. Show what has been reviewed, what each alternative offers and why your chosen path makes sense.
