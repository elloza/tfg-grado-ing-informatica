# Requirements, scope, and constraints

The reader should understand what is inside and outside the TFG. The DIA v2 guide recommends summarizing initial requirements, hypotheses, constraints, scope, expected impact and the alternatives and feasibility study in the report {cite:p}`dia_documentacion_tfg_v2`.

## Initial requirements

The report does not need to repeat the whole A1 annex. It should present a summary that makes the commitment clear:

- main functional requirements;
- relevant non-functional requirements;
- use cases, user stories or equivalent format;
- acceptance criteria;
- data, security, performance, accessibility, deployment or maintenance constraints.

Mandatory requirements should be written as commitments: "the system must...", "the application shall...". Use "should" for desirable improvements outside the minimum scope.

## Hypotheses, constraints and scope

Constraints are not excuses; they are design conditions. They may come from available time, data, regulations, privacy, hardware, budget, APIs, prior knowledge, integration needs or limited access to users.

The scope should make clear:

- what the TFG includes;
- what is outside the work;
- what is left as future work;
- what level of completion is expected: prototype, usable product, research work, library, demonstrator or technical documentation.

It is also useful to explain the expected impact: which user, organization, process or sector benefits from the solution and how.

## Alternatives and feasibility

The report should show that technologies were not chosen randomly. Compare alternatives for languages, frameworks, APIs, databases, models or services. The comparison can be brief, but it should justify the choice using criteria such as cost, maturity, documentation, performance, privacy, deployment, community, license or compatibility with requirements.

When applicable, add a qualitative assessment of costs and benefits. In a TFG, monetization is not always relevant, but effort, maintenance, reuse and real feasibility usually are.
