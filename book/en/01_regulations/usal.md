# USAL Regulations

The University of Salamanca regulations define the common framework for all Final Degree Projects: the nature of the TFG, enrollment, organization, assessment, and public defense. The Faculty and the Department adapt that framework to each degree.

For a Computer Engineering student, the important point is not to read the general regulations as an isolated document. They help explain shared principles:

- The TFG is a compulsory subject in the curriculum.
- The defense takes place in a public session.
- Assessment follows the regulations of the center and the degree.
- The student must meet the academic requirements before defending the project.

The Faculty page links to the University of Salamanca Final Degree Project Regulations, approved by the Governing Council on May 27, 2022 and amended on March 24, 2023 {cite:p}`usal_tfg_reglamento_2023`.

## What Should the Student Do?

1. Check the regulations in force for the corresponding academic year.
2. Review whether there are changes in the calendar, submission, or defense process.
3. Confirm any administrative requirement questions with the supervisor or TFG coordinator.
4. Do not copy regulatory fragments into the report unless they add something specific; complying with them is usually enough.

The report must demonstrate the work carried out, not become a compilation of regulations.
