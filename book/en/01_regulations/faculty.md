## Schedule

The annual schedule is critical because it sets the deadlines for enrollment, proposal, assignment, submission, supervisor report, defense, and closing of minutes. Each year, the Faculty publishes a schedule document that distinguishes general actions from specific deadlines for the Computer Engineering degree {cite:p}`fciencias_temporizacion_2025_2026`.

```{warning}
Do not use dates from previous academic years as an operational reference. This book can explain the process, but the real dates must be checked every year.
```

## Submission and Defense

The Faculty states that submission of the report and the presentation document is carried out through Studium within the established dates. From there, the committee assigns the evaluation panel or committee as applicable.

For documentation, this has a direct consequence: the report and the presentation are not prepared at the end. **They are prepared with enough time for the supervisor to review them and for the student to make corrections before submission**. There are usually several rounds of review and correction. Based on experience from previous years, the review and correction process **usually takes approximately one month**. Do not start preparing the documentation at the last minute, because it is very likely that there will not be enough time to correct it and prepare the final version for submission.
