# Department Regulations

The Department of Computer Science and Automation page (Diaweb) for the TFG in the Computer Engineering degree is the main living source for this degree {cite:p}`dia_tfg_informatica_2025_2026`.

It explains practical aspects such as:

- The nature of the TFG as an integrative exercise.
- Enrollment and requirements for defense.
- Choosing or proposing a topic.
- Approval of proposals by the TFG Committee.
- Preparation and submission of documentation.
- Defense, presentation times, and questions.
- General grading criteria.
- Templates and useful links.

## Documentation

Diaweb states that, in order to defend the TFG, the student must submit the documentation and the artifacts resulting from the work: source code, binaries, libraries, datasets, or other relevant materials.

Regarding the report, it links to the documentation guide adapted for Computer Engineering TFGs. That guide takes as its reference an academic subset of the technical project standards officially disseminated by CPIICM and adapts it to the needs of the degree {cite:p}`dia_documentacion_tfg_v2,cpiicm_normativa_proyectos_2026,cpiicm_nt_v2`.

```{important}
The adapted guide should not be read as a blind template. It must be adapted to the nature of the TFG, and the omission of documents or sections that are not relevant must be justified.
```

## Assessment

The Department summarizes general assessment criteria: quality of the report and annexes, complexity and command of the topic, oral and written presentation, quality of prototypes when applicable, supervisor report, and other criteria considered by the panel.

The Faculty regulations include a rubric that the panel must use to evaluate each part of the report, the presentation, and the work as a whole. This rubric is based on criteria such as technical quality, clarity, rigor, originality, justification of decisions, and other aspects related to Computer Engineering {cite:p}`fciencias_rubrica_tfgii_v12_2022`.

The rubric can be summarized as follows:

| Assessed area | Weight in the grade | Assessment criteria | Minimum to average |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| Report assessment | 25% | Complete, coherent, and detailed statement of objectives, presentation of results, and conclusions; correct description of the technical part, including annexes, design, methodology, and assessments; correctness of format and structure, including tables, diagrams, figures, bibliography, and writing. | 1.0 |
| Oral defense assessment | 15% | Presentation and oral communication; clear, understandable, and ordered explanation of ideas and concepts; discussion and defense of the work. | 0.6 |
| Technical assessment of the TFG | 60% | Final quality of the work; degree of difficulty and alignment with the proposed objectives; correct use of methodologies and techniques. | 2.4 |

The minimum required to average is 40% in each area. The sum of those minimums is 4.0 points out of the final grade.
